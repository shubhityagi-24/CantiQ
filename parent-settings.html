💳 Canteen Balance

Set the amount available for your child's school canteen purchases. This is the starting/prepaid balance, not the live available balance.

This amount is used as the starting balance for purchases.
Your child cannot spend more than this amount in one day.
✓ Settings saved successfully

ℹ️ How the balance works

Annual / Prepaid Balance = starting amount

Available Balance = starting balance − completed purchases

Example: If the starting balance is ₹5,000 and your child purchases a ₹60 Fruit Juice, the available balance becomes ₹4,940 automatically.
